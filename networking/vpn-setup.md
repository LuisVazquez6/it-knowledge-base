# VPN Setup Guide

## Overview
A VPN (Virtual Private Network) allows employees to securely connect to the company network from a remote location. This guide covers installation, connection, and common troubleshooting steps.

## Applies To
- All remote employees
- Windows 10 and Windows 11 Devices
- Company-iisues and approved personal Devices

---

# Step 1 - Download and install VPN Client 

1. Navigate to the **company IT portal** or the download link provided by your IT department.
2. Download the **VPN client installer** for your OS (Operating System)
3. Run the installer and follow the on-screen prompts.
4. **Restart your computer** after installation is complete

---

## Step 2 - Configure the VPN

1. Open the VPN client
2. Select your saved connection profile
3. Enter your **company username and password**
4. Clikc **Connect**
5. Wait for the status to change to **Connected**
6. You can now access internal company resources as if you were in the office.

---

## Step 4 - Dissconnect from the VPN

1. Open the VPN client
2. Click **Disconnect**
3. Confirm your are disconnected before resuming personal browsing

---

## Common VPN Errors & Fixes

| Error | Likely Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Connection timed out | Server unreachable or firewall blocking | Check internet connection; contact IT if persistent |
| Invalid credentials | Wrong username or password | Verify credentials; reset password if needed |
| VPN connects but no access to resources | Split tunnel configuration issue | Contact IT — may need full tunnel mode enabled |
| VPN client won't launch | Corrupted installation | Uninstall and reinstall the VPN client |
| Two-factor authentication failure | MFA app out of sync | Re-sync MFA app or contact IT for a reset |

---

## Escalation

Escalate to **Tier 2** if:
- The VPN connects but internal resources are still unreachable
- Certificate errors appear during connection
- The issue affects multiple remote users simultaneously
- VPN access needs to be provisioned for the first time
